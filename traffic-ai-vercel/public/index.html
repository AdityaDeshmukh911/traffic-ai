1. The Problem & The Idea

Traditional fixed-time signals give North–South and East–West the exact same green time (20 s each), even when 30 cars wait on one road and 2 on the other. TRAFFIC AI scores both directions every second and shifts green time toward higher demand while guaranteeing fairness and safety.

Score = 60% × (cars waiting ÷ 25) + 40% × (seconds since green ÷ 60)
  • • Minimum green: 15 s before any switch is considered.
  • • Safety clearance: Every change passes through 3 s Yellow and 2 s All-Red.
  • • Maximum green: Forced switch at 40 s so no road is ever starved.

3. Two-Minute Presentation Script

"Most city traffic lights still run on fixed timers—even when one road has 30 cars and the cross street has 2. In TRAFFIC AI, we built a repeatable 3×3 city simulator where both a fixed-time controller and a smart adaptive controller face the exact same seeded traffic. Every second, the smart signal scores each direction by queue length and waiting time, and never compromises safety: every switch goes through 3 seconds of yellow and 2 seconds of all-red, with a 40-second maximum wait cap. On quiet and normal days, it cuts average wait by 20–26%, while honestly showing that gains shrink to 1–2% in heavy gridlock. You can also dispatch an ambulance using Dijkstra's algorithm, trigger road incidents that cut capacity, and export the full run to CSV."
